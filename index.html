    What the data says

    TV and radio drive sales. TV has the strongest link to sales (correlation 0.78), followed by radio (0.58).

    Newspaper adds almost nothing. Once TV and radio are accounted for, newspaper spend has close to zero effect.

    The channels work together. Tree-based models beat linear regression by a wide margin, so the effect of one channel depends on the others.

    How it was built

    1. Loaded the public Advertising dataset: 200 rows, no missing values.
    2. Explored correlations and spend-vs-sales relationships.
    3. Split the data 80/20 into training and test sets.
    4. Trained Linear Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting.
    5. Compared them with R², MAE, RMSE and 5-fold cross-validation.
    6. Used the best model to predict sales for new budgets.
    Built with Python, pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib and seaborn. Source: sales_prediction.py
